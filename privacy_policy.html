Al Quran & Dua App - Read & Explore Privacy Policy

by bmiaji · Published January 21, 2025 · Updated January 13, 2026

1. Overview

Your privacy is important to us. This privacy policy explains how the Al Quran & Dua App handles your information and ensures transparency in how your data is treated.

2. Permissions Requested

This app may request the following permissions:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: To inform you about new features or Quran-related content such as reminders or audio download status.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: To allow seamless playback of Quranic audio even when the app is minimized.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC: To support background synchronization of downloadable content.
  • Storage Access: To save downloaded Quran audio for offline playback.

Note: We do not request access to sensitive permissions like location, contacts, camera, or microphone.

3. Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, usage data, or device identifiers.

The app functions fully offline, and any data downloaded (e.g., audio) remains only on your device and is not transmitted to us or any third party.

4. Third-Party Services

This app does not use analytics tools, advertising SDKs, or other third-party libraries that collect or track user behavior.

5. Children’s Privacy

This app is designed to be safe and accessible for all age groups, including children. Since we do not collect any data, there is no risk to child privacy.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Any changes will be posted within the app or on this page, with an updated effective date.
